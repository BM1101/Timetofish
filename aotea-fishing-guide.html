Tides

Bite times solunar

Major periods run for two hours around the moon passing overhead and underfoot; minor periods for one hour around moonrise and moonset. The half hour either side of every high and low tide is cut out — the bite tends to switch off through the turn — so a window is trimmed or split around it, and one falling entirely on the turn is marked slack. A window that lands on dawn or dusk is the one to plan around.

Sun & moon

Times are New Zealand local time (NZST/NZDT). Sun, moon, solunar and maramataka are computed on your device for the spot you choose. Tide predictions come from the NIWA Tide API (heights above chart datum) and are not official tide tables; allow for weather, barometric pressure and local effects, especially on the exposed east coast.

The maramataka shown is a widely published thirty-night version. Iwi and hapū of Aotea and the wider Hauraki Gulf keep their own; where they differ, theirs is the one to follow.
